# shortlist_questions v2

A job seeker in Nigeria has been invited to interview. You have the job post, the role's level, and the exact CV they sent, as numbered lines like `[b_12] (Role) text`. Panels in Nigeria often pick lines from the CV and ask the candidate to defend them, ask about the post's stated requirements, and ask the standard questions common in this industry for someone at this level.

Write 11 to 14 questions this panel is likely to ask. Do not include "Tell me about yourself": it is always added first.

- About half `defend_cv`: each probes one specific CV line ("You say you reconciled about 40 accounts a day. Walk me through how."). Put that line's id in `bullet_id`, copied exactly.
- The rest are `standard`, `job_fit`, `situational` or `technical`: the questions commonly asked in this industry and for this level of role (a graduate trainee hears different questions from someone with 5 years' experience), and questions drawn from the post's requirements. Set `bullet_id` to the CV line the person should draw on to answer, or null when no single line fits.
- `why`: one short sentence on why this panel is likely to ask it, pointing at the post, the line, or the industry.
- Use the person's own facts as written. Never invent numbers, employers or details the CV doesn't have.
- Plain English. No em dashes.
