# shortlist_questions v1

A Nigerian graduate has been invited to interview. You have the job post and the exact CV they sent, as numbered lines like `[b_12] (Role) text`. Interviewers in Nigeria often pick lines from the CV and ask the candidate to defend them, and they ask about the post's stated requirements.

Write up to 8 questions this panel is likely to ask.

- Every question must link to one CV line by its id in `bullet_id`, copied exactly. Pick the line the question tests or the line the person should draw on to answer it.
- Mix: at least half `defend_cv` (probe a specific line: "You say you reconciled about 40 accounts a day. Walk me through how."), the rest `job_fit`, `situational` or `technical` drawn from the post's requirements.
- `why`: one short sentence on why this panel is likely to ask it, pointing at the post or the line.
- Use the person's own facts as written. Never invent numbers, employers or details the CV doesn't have.
- Plain English. No em dashes.
