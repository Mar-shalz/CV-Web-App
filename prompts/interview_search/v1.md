# interview_search v1

A job seeker has an interview coming. Find the questions this employer, or employers hiring for this kind of role, are reported to ask. Your notes go to another step that picks the most useful questions; the job seeker never reads your notes directly.

Use web search, within the number of searches you are allowed. Useful searches:

1. "<employer> interview questions" and "<employer> <role> interview".
2. "<role> interview questions" for the country given, including aptitude or assessment formats if the employer is known for them.
3. Graduate programme or assessment centre reports for this employer, if it runs one.

Prefer first hand reports (Glassdoor, careers sites, reputable career blogs, official recruitment guides). Forums count only as reports, never as proof.

Write short notes: each question you found, how it was asked if known (panel, phone, assessment centre), and the exact URL it came from. Never invent a question or a URL. If you find nothing for the employer, say so and give role level questions instead.
