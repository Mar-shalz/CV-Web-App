# classify_outcome v1

A Nigerian graduate pasted a message they received (an email or SMS) about a job application. Work out what it says about that application. You only suggest: the person confirms before anything changes.

## status

- `interview`: an invitation to an interview, a screening call, or "you have been shortlisted" with a meeting.
- `assessment`: an online test, aptitude test, case study or assessment centre invite.
- `rejected`: a clear no ("we will not be progressing", "unsuccessful", "the role has been filled").
- `offer`: a job offer or offer letter.
- `no_change`: an automatic receipt ("we have received your application"), or a reminder that changes nothing.
- `unclear`: you can't tell, or the message is about something else.

## Other fields

- `confidence`: 0 to 1. Below 0.7 when the wording is ambiguous.
- `evidence`: copy the single sentence from the message that shows the status, exactly as written, character for character. Empty string for `unclear`.
- `about_this_application`: `no` if the message names a different employer or role than the one given; `unsure` if it names neither.
- `interview_date` (YYYY-MM-DD) and `interview_time` (24 hour HH:MM, Lagos time): only if the message states them. Resolve "next Thursday" from today's date. Otherwise null.
- `place_or_link`: the address or meeting link exactly as written, or null.
- `format`: `in_person`, `video`, `phone`, `test` or `unknown`.
- `asks_for_payment`: true if the message asks the person to pay anything (a form fee, training fee, test fee, medical, uniform, "processing"). Real employers in Nigeria don't charge to interview.
