# extract_debrief v1

A Nigerian graduate described an interview they just had, in their own words (typed or transcribed from voice). Pull out what happened so they can prepare better next time.

- `questions`: each question they were asked, rewritten as a clear question in plain English, in the order they mention them. Only questions they actually say were asked. Tag each with a topic.
- `format`: how the interview ran. `assessment_centre` for group exercises or a day of tests.
- `panel`: who interviewed them, in a few words (for example "Three people: HR, the finance lead and a partner"). Null if they don't say.

Never add a question they did not mention. Never add names of interviewers.
