# Market review: who else does this, and how we win

Founder round 16, 15 October 2026. Desk research from public sources (vendor sites, review
roundups, news). Prices move often and several roundups disagree, so treat figures as
ranges. Sources are listed at the end.

## What we do, in one line
Truthful, tailored applications for early career job seekers: a CV written only from your
confirmed experience (every line traceable), a fit check before you spend time, a scam check
on the job, the exact CV and post kept with each application, and interview prep from what
you actually sent.

## Who does parts of it

| Group | Examples | What they do well | What they don't do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Global AI CV tailoring | Teal, Rezi, Jobscan, Kickresume, Enhancv, Four-Leaf, Careerflow | Keyword matching, ATS scoring, templates, job trackers (Teal), LinkedIn polish (Careerflow) | No truth check on what the AI writes; no scam check; priced in dollars ($20 to $50 a month); built for US and EU job markets |

## Where we're already ahead

1. **Truth, not just polish.** Every CV line comes from confirmed experience and passes a fact
   lock; untraced words are flagged before download. Recruiters are reacting against invented
   AI CVs (Gartner's projection that 1 in 4 candidate profiles will be fake by 2028 is widely
   quoted; surveys report most recruiters have seen AI fabricated applications). "Every line
   is yours" is a position nobody in the table owns.
2. **The scam check is real, and free.** Company record, company age, the company's own
   hiring page, the email domain and public warnings, in the app and now in the browser panel.
   Job scams are rising fast (US FTC reported losses grew from $90m in 2020 to $501m in 2024),
   and fake job offers are a well known problem in Nigeria.
3. **Built for Nigeria first, without being only Nigerian.** Naira pricing (a ₦5,000 pass
   versus $29 a month elsewhere), NYSC and SIWES as real experience, Nigerian and UK formats,
   low data use, voice input.
4. **One loop, not five tools.** Fit check, scam check, tailored CV, the email pack, the record
   of what was sent, check ins and interview prep from that exact CV. Competitors cover one or
   two of these.
5. **Interview prep tied to your own CV.** Questions come from the job and the lines you sent,
   so practice can't drift into claims you can't back.

## Where we're behind (and the fix)

- **Distribution.** Jobberman and Fuzu own the traffic. Fix: campus and NYSC CDS partnerships,
  WhatsApp sharing of the scam check ("Check this job before you apply"), creator partnerships
  with Nigerian career influencers, and the browser panel for laptop users.
- **Job supply.** We don't list jobs. Fix: don't build a board; read any board (links, the
  panel, share to the app). Later, partner with boards to add "Tailor with proof" buttons.
- **Templates.** Rivals offer dozens. Fix: four ATS safe designs now (Classic, Modern,
  Executive, Signature); add more only when data shows people leave for design.
- **Proof that it works.** Fix: measure and publish interview rates for people who used us
  (with consent), and a public "verified employers" list from the scam check.
- **Brand and domain.** "Receipts" is a working name. Fix: settle the name (Trail was the
  recommendation) before spending on marketing.

## How to win the category

1. **Own "proof".** One promise everywhere: every line on your CV can be backed. Add a small
   "Checked by Receipts: every line traced to the candidate's own record" footer option
   people can switch on, which recruiters will start to recognise.
2. **Lead with the free scam check.** It's the thing people share. Make every check shareable
   (a link with the verdict, no personal data), especially on WhatsApp and X.
3. **Win NYSC and final year students.** They're the largest group with thin CVs and the most
   exposure to scams. Talks at CDS, SIWES offices and career fairs; a student price.
4. **Employer side, later.** Recruiters drowning in AI CVs will pay for candidates whose lines
   are traceable. A "verified application" link for employers is the long term moat and a
   second revenue line.
5. **Keep the price local and simple.** ₦5,000 a month with a clear CV allowance beats dollar
   subscriptions and surprise renewals (a common complaint about Final Round AI).
6. **Measure the loop.** Interviews per 10 applications, scam checks run, scam reports caught,
   CVs downloaded with zero open flags. Publish the good ones.
