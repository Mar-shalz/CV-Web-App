# Market review: who else does this, and how we win

Founder round 16, 15 October 2026. Desk research from public sources (vendor sites, review
roundups, news). Prices move often and several roundups disagree, so treat figures as
ranges. Sources are listed at the end.

## What we do, in one line
Truthful, tailored applications for early career job seekers: a CV written only from your
confirmed experience (every line traceable), a fit check before you spend time, a scam check
on the job, the exact CV and post kept with each application, and interview prep from what
you actually sent.

## Who does parts of it

| Group | Examples | What they do well | What they don't do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Global AI CV tailoring | Teal, Rezi, Jobscan, Kickresume, Enhancv, Four-Leaf, Careerflow | Keyword matching, ATS scoring, templates, job trackers (Teal), LinkedIn polish (Careerflow) | No truth check on what the AI writes; no scam check; priced in dollars ($20 to $50 a month); built for US and EU job markets |
| Job boards in Nigeria and Africa | Jobberman, MyJobMag, Fuzu, BrighterMonday | Huge job supply, CV upload and parsing (Jobberman), AI matching and a CV builder (Fuzu) | Generic CVs, no tailoring per job, no record of what you sent, limited fraud tooling |
| Local career tools | MY CV CREATOR and similar | Local brand, coaching, interview prep | Small, mostly templates and human services, no evidence trail |
| Interview practice | Final Round AI, Big Interview, Yoodli, Pramp | Mock interviews, speech coaching | Not tied to the CV you sent; $10 to $150 a month; Final Round's live "copilot" is banned by many employers |
| Scam checkers | Fake Job Shield (Chrome), hackathon tools (JobShield, JobSignal), campaign bots | Rule based red flags | Mostly prototypes; few check company records, domains and the careers page together |
| Application autofill | Simplify, Huntr and similar | Fills forms, tracks applications | Speed over quality; nothing on truth or scams |

## Where we're already ahead

1. **Truth, not just polish.** Every CV line comes from confirmed experience and passes a fact
   lock; untraced words are flagged before download. Recruiters are reacting against invented
   AI CVs (Gartner's projection that 1 in 4 candidate profiles will be fake by 2028 is widely
   quoted; surveys report most recruiters have seen AI fabricated applications). "Every line
   is yours" is a position nobody in the table owns.
2. **The scam check is real, and free.** Company record, company age, the company's own
   hiring page, the email domain and public warnings, in the app and now in the browser panel.
   Job scams are rising fast (US FTC reported losses grew from $90m in 2020 to $501m in 2024),
   and fake job offers are a well known problem in Nigeria.
3. **Built for Nigeria first, without being only Nigerian.** Naira pricing (a ₦5,000 pass
   versus $29 a month elsewhere), NYSC and SIWES as real experience, Nigerian and UK formats,
   low data use, voice input.
4. **One loop, not five tools.** Fit check, scam check, tailored CV, the email pack, the record
   of what was sent, check ins and interview prep from that exact CV. Competitors cover one or
   two of these.
5. **Interview prep tied to your own CV.** Questions come from the job and the lines you sent,
   so practice can't drift into claims you can't back.

## Where we're behind (and the fix)

- **Distribution.** Jobberman and Fuzu own the traffic. Fix: campus and NYSC CDS partnerships,
  WhatsApp sharing of the scam check ("Check this job before you apply"), creator partnerships
  with Nigerian career influencers, and the browser panel for laptop users.
- **Job supply.** We don't list jobs. Fix: don't build a board; read any board (links, the
  panel, share to the app). Later, partner with boards to add "Tailor with proof" buttons.
- **Templates.** Rivals offer dozens. Fix: four ATS safe designs now (Classic, Modern,
  Executive, Signature); add more only when data shows people leave for design.
- **Proof that it works.** Fix: measure and publish interview rates for people who used us
  (with consent), and a public "verified employers" list from the scam check.
- **Brand and domain.** "Receipts" is a working name. Fix: settle the name (Trail was the
  recommendation) before spending on marketing.

## How to win the category

1. **Own "proof".** One promise everywhere: every line on your CV can be backed. Add a small
   "Checked by Receipts: every line traced to the candidate's own record" footer option
   people can switch on, which recruiters will start to recognise.
2. **Lead with the free scam check.** It's the thing people share. Make every check shareable
   (a link with the verdict, no personal data), especially on WhatsApp and X.
3. **Win NYSC and final year students.** They're the largest group with thin CVs and the most
   exposure to scams. Talks at CDS, SIWES offices and career fairs; a student price.
4. **Employer side, later.** Recruiters drowning in AI CVs will pay for candidates whose lines
   are traceable. A "verified application" link for employers is the long term moat and a
   second revenue line.
5. **Keep the price local and simple.** ₦5,000 a month with a clear CV allowance beats dollar
   subscriptions and surprise renewals (a common complaint about Final Round AI).
6. **Measure the loop.** Interviews per 10 applications, scam checks run, scam reports caught,
   CVs downloaded with zero open flags. Publish the good ones.

## AIApply and Totaljobs (founder round 20, 16 October 2026)

A UX research read: what each product promises, the job it does for the user, where users
get hurt, and whether we compete head on. Desk research only (their sites, review roundups,
Trustpilot and BBB summaries); no interviews yet, so treat it as hypotheses to test.

### AIApply (aiapply.co)
- **What it is:** a UK registered AI job application suite. Auto Apply (finds jobs and
  submits tailored applications for you, sold as credits on top of a subscription), an AI
  resume builder and scanner, cover letters, mock interviews, "Interview Buddy" (live answer
  suggestions during a real interview), a resume translator and a job board. Priced in
  dollars; reported from about $12 to $23 a week or about $29 a month, plus credit packs.
- **The job it does for users:** volume. "Apply to more jobs with less effort."
- **Where users get hurt (from reviews):** CVs with skills, experience or languages the person
  doesn't have; applications to wrong or unsuitable jobs (wrong work authorisation, the
  person's current employer); auto apply not in the base plan; refund and billing complaints
  (an F rating on BBB, a Trustpilot warning about misleading display of reviews). Its site
  says employers generally can't tell AI was used; it makes no claim about keeping CVs true.
- **Same as us?** No. Overlap: tailored CVs, cover letters, interview practice. Opposite on
  the things that matter most to us: it sends applications for you (we never do), it doesn't
  tie each line to evidence (we fact lock every line), it has no scam check, and it is priced
  in dollars for US and UK markets.
- **Our advantage:** truth (every line traceable, suggestions only take effect when accepted),
  the free scam check, a record of exactly what was sent, interview prep from that exact CV,
  Naira pricing and Nigerian formats (NYSC, SIWES, referees). Their weakness is our pitch:
  "Nothing goes out without you, and nothing on your CV is made up."
- **Where they're ahead:** speed and volume, a job feed, 13 interface languages, live
  interview help (which many employers treat as cheating; we won't build it).

### Totaljobs (totaljobs.com)
- **What it is:** one of the UK's largest job boards (part of the Stepstone Group): job
  search, job alerts, CV upload so recruiters can find you, company profiles, a salary
  checker and careers advice. Free to job seekers; employers pay.
- **The job it does for users:** supply. "Find UK jobs and be found by UK recruiters."
- **Same as us?** No. It's a place to find jobs, not a tool to decide, tailor, check and
  record. It doesn't tailor a CV to each advert, check whether an advert is real, keep the
  CV you sent, or prepare you from it. Scammers also borrow job board names in fake offers.
- **Relationship:** a source, not a rival. People will find jobs on Totaljobs and bring them
  to us (link, the browser extension, or a paste). Totaljobs is in our known job sites list,
  so the scam check recognises it. For Nigerians aiming at UK roles, we add what Totaljobs
  doesn't: a UK format CV, NYSC and HND explained, and a check that the job is real.

### What to test with users next
1. Do Nigerian graduates who tried auto apply tools trust them less after a bad application?
   (Hypothesis: yes, and "nothing goes out without you" is a reason to choose us.)
2. Is the match percentage on the fit check read as a promise? (Watch for people skipping
   jobs below 50% that they'd get.)
3. Do people accept suggested lines one by one or use "Accept all"? (If mostly "Accept
   all", the suggestions are trusted; if mostly "Keep my words", the writing is off.)
4. How many people add referees, and do Nigerian employers ask for them at the CV stage?
